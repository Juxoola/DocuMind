**How to Make a “Smoking” Realistic Candle (Using a Candle + a Small Smoke‑Generating Device)**  
*(A step‑by‑step guide for a hobbyist or a stage‑craft enthusiast)*  

---

### 1. What you’ll need  
| Item | Quantity | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Candle (paraffin or soy) | 1 × 12 in. (or any length you prefer) | Choose a thick‑bodied candle for better heat retention. |
| Small smoke‑generating device | 1 | e.g., a miniature propane burner, a small electric heater, or a smoke‑generator kit. |
| Heat‑resistant glass or metal tube | 1 | Acts as a “smoke chimney” and protects the candle. |
| matches or lighter | 1 | For lighting the candle. |
| propane or butane (if using a small burner) | 1 × 100 ml | Or an electric coil if you prefer an electric heater. |
| Optional: a small amount of “smoke‑oil” (e.g., lamp oil) | 1 × 30 ml | Adds extra realism to the smoke. |
| Safety gear |  | Gloves, goggles, fire extinguisher. |

---

### 2. Setting up the device  
1. **Prepare the chimney** – Clean the inside of the glass/metal tube. Make sure it’s smooth; a rough surface will trap smoke.  
2. **Insert the candle** – Place the candle at the bottom of the tube, leaving a few centimeters of space between the candle’s top and the tube’s rim.  
3. **Attach the smoke‑generator** – If you’re using a propane burner, attach it to the side of the tube just above the candle. If you’re using an electric heater, plug it into the same spot.  
4. **Seal the system** – Use a small amount of silicone or a heat‑resistant glue to seal the junction between the candle and the tube, ensuring no leaks.
